Safety First

CSAE Protection Standards

Zero Tolerance Implementation

01 Our Commitment

NotiBee is dedicated to providing a safe environment for all members of the Hive. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Protecting the safety and well-being of minors is our highest priority.

02 Strictly Prohibited Content

The following activities are strictly prohibited within the NotiBee Hive:

Illegal Imagery: The uploading, sharing, or distribution of any imagery depicting child sexual abuse or exploitation.
Grooming Behavior: Any attempt to solicit or engage in manipulative behavior with minors for exploitative purposes.
Promotional Content: Sharing links or references to external sites containing CSAE materials.

03 Detection and Prevention

To ensure the Hive remains safe, we employ various measures:

Hashing & Matching: If content is uploaded, it may be compared against known databases of illegal materials (using standard hashing technology) to prevent the spread of CSAE.
Manual Reporting: Our built-in reporting systems allow any bee to flag suspicious behavior or content immediately for review by our safety team.

04 Law Enforcement Cooperation

Reporting to Authorities

Any identified instances of CSAE will be reported directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and appropriate global law enforcement agencies. We cooperate fully with authorities to ensure individuals responsible are held accountable.

05 Enforcement Actions

Violators of these standards will face immediate and permanent termination of their Bee ID. There is no appeal process for violations related to the safety of minors.
