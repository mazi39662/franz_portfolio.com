Privacy Policy

Application: The Fold (RSS Reader) |

Last Updated: January 07, 2026

At The Fold, we prioritize the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by The Fold and how we use it. If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.

1. Information We Collect

The Fold is designed to be a privacy-first RSS news aggregator. We want to be clear about the data we do (and do not) handle:

  • Personal Information: The Fold does not require user registration. We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Usage Data: We may collect non-personal information about how you interact with the app, such as the number of articles read or the frequency of app usage, to improve our services.
  • Subscription Data: Your RSS feed subscriptions and favorited articles are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

2. RSS Feeds and External Links

The Fold allows you to subscribe to third-party RSS feeds. Please be aware that:

  • When you view an article, the content is fetched from the respective publisher's server.
  • Clicking on "Read More" or external links will direct you to the publisher's website.
  • We do not have control over, and are not responsible for, the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

The minimal data handled by the app is used solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the core functionality of the RSS reader.
  • Improve user experience through local personalization.
  • Ensure the technical stability of the application.

4. Data Security

We use standard security measures to protect any data stored locally on your device. However, remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. The Fold does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our app, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the developer through the contact section of the Portfolio Chronicle.
